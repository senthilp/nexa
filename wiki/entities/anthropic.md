# Anthropic

AI safety and research company focused on building reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems.

## Context in Sources

**Creator of Claude**: Anthropic developed the Claude model family (Sonnet 4.5, Opus 4.5, etc.) and associated infrastructure.

**Managed Agents platform**: Built hosted service for running long-horizon AI agents, described in [[Scaling Managed Agents: Decoupling the brain from the hands]].

**Engineering philosophy**: Publishes engineering blog covering agent design, harness patterns, context engineering, and infrastructure decisions. Emphasizes that harness assumptions "go stale" as models improve, requiring flexible architectures.
